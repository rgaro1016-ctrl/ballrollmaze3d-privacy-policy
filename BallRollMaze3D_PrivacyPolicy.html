Privacy Policy – BallRollMaze3D

Last updated: November 19, 2026

BallRollMaze3D is a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information.

Based on the current version of BallRollMaze3D, the developer does not intentionally collect, store, or request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts.

Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress or settings, if saved, may be stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer by the game.

Third-Party Services

The current version is not intended to use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If third-party services are added in a future update, this policy will be updated to explain their data practices.

Children's Privacy

BallRollMaze3D does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer, contact us so we can review the concern.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the game’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details provided on the game's Google Play store listing.
